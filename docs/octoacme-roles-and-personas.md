# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own quality assurance strategy, test planning, and acceptance criteria validation. They ensure that features meet quality standards and acceptance criteria before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define test plans and QA approach for features and releases
- Create and maintain test cases and acceptance criteria checklists
- Coordinate manual and automated testing efforts
- Identify quality gaps and blockers during execution
- Validate features against acceptance criteria before handoff to production
- Report quality metrics and test coverage

### Goals
- Ensure all features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards
- Reduce production defects and regressions
- Provide clear quality signals to enable confident releases

### Typical Communication
- Weekly QA sync with development and product teams
- Test status updates in daily standups
- Quality metrics and risk reporting in weekly stakeholder updates

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Works with Developers**: Validates code against acceptance criteria, reports defects for fixes
- **Works with Product Managers**: Confirms acceptance criteria clarity, prioritizes testing efforts
- **Works with Project Managers**: Reports quality status, identifies testing blockers for escalation

---

## Technical Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads drive technical strategy, design decisions, and architecture for features and projects. They balance technical excellence with delivery speed and provide guidance on technical risks and trade-offs.

### Responsibilities
- Lead technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Define technical standards and best practices for the project
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigation strategies
- Mentor developers and support technical problem-solving
- Ensure scalability, security, and maintainability of solutions

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound, maintainable solutions
- Reduce technical debt while meeting delivery timelines
- Enable team learning and growth in technical capabilities

### Typical Communication
- Technical design discussions and code reviews
- Architecture decision records (ADRs) when needed
- Technical risk reporting in weekly syncs

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Works with Developers**: Provides technical guidance, reviews design and code, mentors on best practices
- **Works with Project Managers**: Escalates technical risks and blockers, advises on timeline impact of technical decisions
- **Works with Product Managers**: Advises on technical feasibility and trade-offs for features

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, approval authority, and executive oversight. They ensure projects align with business strategy and remove organizational blockers.

### Responsibilities
- Define business requirements and success metrics
- Provide approval for scope, timeline, and go/no-go decisions
- Remove organizational and cross-team blockers
- Provide regular feedback and course corrections
- Communicate project status to executive leadership

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver measurable business value
- Maintain alignment between delivery and business strategy
- Minimize organizational friction and blockers

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder updates and demos
- Decision gates at key milestones
- Ad-hoc escalation and unblocking

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Works with Project Managers**: Provides business context, approves scope/timeline changes, removes blockers
- **Works with Product Managers**: Aligns on business priorities, validates success metrics
- **Works with Developers**: Provides feedback on deliverables, communicates business value

---

## Scrum Master/Facilitator

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Facilitators enable the team to execute effectively by removing impediments, facilitating ceremonies, and supporting continuous improvement. They are guardians of the team's process and well-being.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Remove blockers and impediments to team productivity
- Coach the team on Agile practices and process improvements
- Track and communicate sprint metrics and progress
- Support psychological safety and team health

### Goals
- Maximize team velocity and productivity
- Foster continuous improvement culture
- Ensure ceremonies are effective and team is engaged

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups (facilitation)
- Sprint retrospectives and action item tracking
- Impediment escalation to Project Manager or Product Manager

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Works with Developers**: Removes blockers, protects from distractions, facilitates ceremonies
- **Works with Project Managers**: Escalates impediments, reports on team health and velocity
- **Works with Product Managers**: Facilitates backlog refinement, communicates capacity constraints

---

## Operations/DevOps Engineer

### Role Summary
Operations and DevOps Engineers manage infrastructure, deployment pipelines, and operational support for delivered features. They ensure reliable, secure, and scalable production environments.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain CI/CD pipelines and deployment infrastructure
- Manage production environments and infrastructure as code
- Coordinate deployments and manage rollback procedures
- Monitor system health, performance, and security
- Support incident response and post-incident analysis

### Goals
- Enable fast, reliable, and secure deployments
- Maintain high system availability and performance
- Reduce operational toil and manual effort

### Typical Communication
- Pre-release deployment planning with developers and QA
- Incident response during deployments
- Infrastructure and monitoring updates in weekly syncs

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Works with Developers**: Supports deployment process, provides infrastructure guidance, responds to production issues
- **Works with Project Managers**: Coordinates deployment windows, communicates release readiness
- **Works with QA/Testing Lead**: Provides staging/production environments, supports smoke testing
